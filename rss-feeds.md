## Notes

- Substack-based feeds (e.g. Import AI) tend to include full article text in
  `content:encoded`, which suits this app's RSS-summary-only generation well.
- Press-outlet feeds (e.g. TechCrunch) usually only give a short excerpt, so
  generated posts will lean more on the AI filling gaps than rewriting real
  content.
